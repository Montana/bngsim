- **A size that moves through `rateOf`, or through a cycle of rules that hold, was refused where bngsim can follow its rate** ([#1281](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1281)). Since #1193, `cv := 2 + rateOf(S3)` and `cv := 2 + rateOf(P)` were refused because the rate of a rate was not derived. It now is:
  - for a rate-rule variable, the rate of its right-hand side;
  - for a species in a compartment whose size holds, the rate of its reactions' laws, each times its stoichiometry, over the size.

  A rule on a cycle of rules that hold (#621) has rate 0, and the size's jumps read it by name. Each case now keeps X's amount at A(0)·e^(-t/4), and matches libRoadRunner where its start agrees. Where the rate cannot be followed, the size is still refused by name: an event that moves what the rate reads, a conversion factor, a stoichiometry that moves, `rateOf` of a species whose own size moves, and a cycle of rules or rates that move.
