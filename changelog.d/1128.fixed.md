  state is no longer several percent off with no error.** `X -> Y` at
  `0.01*ceil(0.4*X)*X` from X = 10 came back 5 % off mid-run at rtol 1e-4 and
  1e-6, as `.net` and as SBML. The analytical Jacobian declined these calls
  over the state, and CVODE's difference quotient perturbed X across the step
  at 0.4·10 = 4 and read a slope of about -7e5 for -0.04. Under that matrix the
  corrector kept its prediction for as long as CVODE kept the matrix. The calls
  now differentiate to 0, which they are between their steps, and since #1068
  every step is rooted (SBML's by its GH #244 trigger), so such a model runs on
  the analytical Jacobian. `sgn()` is read as `sign()`. A run with
  sensitivities on `sign()` over the state is no longer refused, and its
  columns match the `if()` spelling across the crossing. The stop such a run
  adds where `sign()` jumps now lists the rate law that holds it, so the
  solver reads the jump from that law: listed as no one's, the jump was judged
  against the largest |dx/dt| in the model, and beside a 1e8-molecule pool
  turning over at 0.1 its saltation term was dropped and dX/dk came back 96 %
  off with no error. Putting the rounding calls back after each derivative no
  longer walks it four times, which cost BIOMD0000000628 1.7 s of its 20 s
  derivation budget. An explicit `jacobian="fd"` still differences across a
  step it starts on. (#1128)
