- **The experimental diffrax solver stops at a switch on the clock (issue
  #865).** `bngsim._diffrax_solver.run_diffrax` integrated straight across a rate
  law such as `if(time() > 1.37, 5, 0.1)`. On the issue's model its error was
  3.4e-3 at `rtol=1e-6`, against CVODE's 7.5e-4, and at `rtol=1e-8` and tighter
  it ran out of steps. The run is now split at the switch times, the crossings
  CVODE stops on, so that no step is taken across a jump, and a piece that ends
  at a switch is solved with Kvaerno3, whose stages stay within its step. The
  same model now has an error of 1.1e-3 in 36 steps at `rtol=1e-6`, and
  completes at `1e-8` and `1e-10` with errors of 9.7e-6 and 4.7e-9 (CVODE's
  are 3.2e-6 and 7.1e-8). A model whose rate laws switch on no clock runs as
  before; a switch only an output reads splits nothing. A rate law
  that switches on the model's state is refused, as events are. So are a
  condition a rate law reads with more crossings than a run may stop at, one no
  resolver can place (a condition on two counters), and a run backward in time
  on a model with a switch on the clock.
