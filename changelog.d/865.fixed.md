- **The experimental diffrax solver stops at a switch on the clock (issue
  #865).** `bngsim._diffrax_solver.run_diffrax` integrated straight across a rate
  law such as `if(time() > 1.37, 5, 0.1)`. On the issue's model its error was
  3.4e-3 at `rtol=1e-6`, against CVODE's 7.5e-4, and at `rtol=1e-8` and tighter
  it ran out of steps. The run is now split at the switch times, the crossings
  CVODE stops on, and each piece reads the rate laws only on its own side of the
  jump. The same model now has an error of 1.1e-3 in 32 steps at `rtol=1e-6`,
  and completes at `1e-8` and `1e-10`: 2.5e-5 and 1.1e-6, diffrax's error on
  the model with no switch. A rate law that switches on the model's state is
  refused, as events are. So is a condition with more crossings than a run may
  stop at.
