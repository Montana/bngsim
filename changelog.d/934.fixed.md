- **An event whose trigger starts exactly on its threshold now fires when the
  trajectory leaves into the trigger's true side, at any order (issue #934).**
  `at (B > 0)` with B(0) = 0 and B = t²/2 never fired: a rule from #340 refused
  the first root of a trigger whose residual started at zero unless the flow
  carried it off at first order, so every second- or higher-order departure was
  dropped without a warning. The rule is gone, and bngsim follows SBML and
  libRoadRunner: the event fires at the start; a trigger that stays on its
  threshold or leaves into its false side still never fires. BIOMD0000000285's
  death events (`PIdeath > 0`, `p38death > 0`) now fire at the start and stop
  the cell, as in libRoadRunner; AMICI, which keeps the old behaviour, now
  disagrees there. Its forward sensitivities are refused by name (#49).
