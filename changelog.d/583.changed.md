- **The C++ core is built with `-Wall -Wextra`, and CI runs the native suite under AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer** ([#583](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/583)). The warnings are informational (not `-Werror`) and leave out `-Wmissing-field-initializers`, which flags the deliberate `T x{a, b}` initializations in `src/`. The sanitizer job starts non-blocking. No behavior changes.
