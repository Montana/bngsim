- **A run or steady state on a differenced Jacobian is no longer wrong where the difference steps across a jump in a rate law** ([#1002](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1002)). With `jacobian="fd"`, or one law the closed form declines, CVODE and KINSOL differenced the right-hand side, and from a state within one step of a threshold of `if`, a comparison or `ceil` the step crossed it: `if(X > 5, 0.04, 0.02)·X` read −1.3e6 for −0.02. A run starting at X = 5 followed its predictor to 166% off (2.1% with a declined law); a species held at 0 under `if(I > 0, …)` took 1e-36 from rounding and switched the law, 490% off in a run and a steady state 5× off with `converged=True`. bngsim now forms those quotients itself, bit for bit as CVODE and KINSOL did, and where a row's step crosses a jump the species is not moving toward, takes the slope behind it. A column of a species not moving in its step's direction costs one more evaluation of f.
