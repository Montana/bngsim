- The Table S4 agreement check (`benchmarks/suites/ode_engines_s4_sbml/check_sbml_engine_agreement.py`) judges agreement against a converged reference, not against bngsim at the timing tolerance, where Koo2013's final state swings by up to 7% with the output grid in every engine (issue #1250). Each engine at rtol 1e-12 is compared with libRoadRunner (bngsim where it fails) species by species, by SBML id, over every output row (COPASI: final state). `agree` is True only when all are within 1e-4; False for one off it or holding a non-finite value; None, with the reason, when species cannot be matched or nothing the reference resolves was compared. Values are compared above 1e3 times the run's absolute tolerance, not a fixed 1e-6. Each engine's error at the figure's tolerances is reported, not judged. The old sorted comparison, which passed a NaN species and could not tell two species apart, is kept only as `bngsim_vs_*_sorted_mre`.
