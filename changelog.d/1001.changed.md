- `steady_state()` and `steady_state_batch()` default to `criterion="scaled"` (issue #1001). `converged` means the Newton step from the state to the root is within each species' tolerance (`rtol` of its size where the solve starts, floored at the smallest initial concentration the model declares), and runs taken on from the state, and from it kicked by a millionth, stay within it. BNG2.pl's `||f||/n < tol`, the old default, accepted the issue's cubic at 0.89 of its root at a scale of 1e-9, BIOMD0000000407 where it starts and BIOMD0000000002 6% from its steady state in a species; it is kept as `criterion="residual"`. Under the default `tol` is not read: a tighter solve takes a smaller `rtol`. An unstable root and a state on a switch's threshold are no longer returned converged; where the step says nothing (a Jacobian singular, or singular but for rounding, as on a continuum of steady states) the runs decide. Results carry `continued_run_distance`.
