- **The parity verdict no longer passes an engine that never moves (issue #873).**
  `deterministic_verdict` judged each species against the largest value in the
  file and against its own peak, so a clamped reservoir, a nearly constant
  species or a large baseline let a frozen trajectory through: before #825 the
  compiled right-hand side froze `catalysis.bngl` at its initial state and the
  nightly scored it PASS with `max_rel 0.0`, and 17 of 592 BNG ODE models pass
  frozen. Every species that moves in the reference is now also held to 1e-2 of
  its range over time, which a frozen engine misses by at least a half and a 4%
  error on one species by at least 4e-2; the worst current PASS in the #873
  census is 7.5e-3. The verdict reports `max_err_over_movement`, and `no_signal`
  where nothing in the reference moves, which the bng, rr and amici rows say.
