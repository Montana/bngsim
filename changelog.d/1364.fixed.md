- **`parse_bngl_protocol` misread five common `simulate`/`parameter_scan` arguments** ([#1364](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1364)), with no lossy flag even under `strict=True`. The arguments are now read as BNG2.pl's `BNGAction.pm` defines them:
  - `continue=>1` starts at the model time, where the previous run ended: (10, 20), not (0, 20).
  - `n_output_steps` is `n_steps`, and defining both is refused.
  - With neither, the default is one step: 2 points, not 101.
  - `sample_times` sets the grid and its end: an even grid from `t_start` is that time course, not a horizon of 0.
  - `par_scan_vals` is the scan: linearly or log-spaced values are the scan's min, max, points and log. Before, the scan collapsed to one run.

  What the protocol cannot hold exactly, an uneven grid or uneven scan values, is now recorded as lossy, and `strict=True` refuses it. The re-emitted actions are calls BNG2.pl accepts, and it writes identical output files from them.
