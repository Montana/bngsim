- **NFsim (`method='nf'` / `'nf_reject'`, `NfsimSession`) refuses a model with
  Arrhenius (energy-based) rate laws, naming the rules.** NFsim's expansion of
  energy rules divides every energy by an `RT` that BioNetGen does not apply
  (2.478 unless the model defines a parameter `RT`), gives a one-way binding
  rule a reverse reaction, and drops one-way unbinding and state-change rules,
  all silently (RuleWorld/nfsim#102). Simulate such a model with
  `method='ode'` or `'ssa'` on the generated network. Energy patterns with no
  Arrhenius rule still run.
