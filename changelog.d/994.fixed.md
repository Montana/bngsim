- **An SBML compartment sized by an assignment rule that reads another rule's
  target loads at its size (issue #994).** Where the compartment's rule came
  first in the document and the model had no `initialAssignment`, the rule read
  the other target unset and the compartment loaded with volume factor 1, so
  every reaction across compartments moved its species by the rate over 1, not
  over the size: `run()` and `steady_state()` ended at `[0.8, 0.4]` for
  `[0.6, 0.3]`, with nothing raised. Assignment rules now settle to a fixed point
  at load in every model, however long the chain.
