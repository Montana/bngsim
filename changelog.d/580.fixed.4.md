- **A kinetic law's local parameters now shadow globals wherever bngsim evaluates the law at load** ([#580](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/580)). Folding a reaction id that an initialAssignment or rule reads, or a `rateOf`, saw no Level 2 local parameter (libsbml lists them elsewhere there) and skipped an unset one, and the floor/ceiling root scan saw no local parameter on either level. A global of the same id stood in, or nothing did: `p = R1` with a local `k = 0.5` and a global `k = 5` gave p = 50 where RoadRunner gives 5, and without the global, or with a global an assignment rule leaves unfoldable, the load refused `p`. These now load at p = 5, and `ceiling(k*A)` gets its roots at the local `k`. In a called function, the same scan also no longer carries an outer formal's time dependence onto a rebound name, or starts a formal it cannot evaluate at load from a global of the same name. An unset local is NaN there, or 0.0 under `BNGSIM_ALLOW_UNSET_PARAMS=1`.
