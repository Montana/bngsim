- **A MIR build reserves 32 MB of address space for each JIT'd model's code, not 128 MB (issue #1215).** Since the MIR refresh in #1208, every live MIR-JIT'd model (a `Simulator`, a steady-state solve, SSA propensities) held 128 MB of address space. That held no memory, but under a cap on the address space (`ulimit -v`, `RLIMIT_AS`) about 60 of them filled 8 GB, and the next allocation failed. The new CMake option `BNGSIM_MIR_CODE_RESERVE_MB` (default 32, at most 2048) sets the size: 32 MB holds the largest committed model's code in one piece (fceri_gamma, 27.9 MB on macOS arm64), and code that outgrows it is mapped on its own and runs the same, as MIR does once its reservation is full. `scripts/rebuild_editable.py` passes it on every rebuild, as it does the feature options (GH #459). Builds without MIR, and Windows, are unchanged.
