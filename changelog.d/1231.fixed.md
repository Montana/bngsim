- **A `.net` reaction line with anything after its rate constant loaded without it, with no error** ([#1231](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1231)). `1 1 2 k delay=tau` loaded as an undelayed reaction and `MM kcat Km delay=tau` as plain MM, where run_network refuses both. The loader now refuses such a line and names the extra tokens, and it refuses `MM kcat` the same way. Two forms that build() refused as an unknown parameter `MM` or `Ele` now say what they are: a stat factor on an MM, Sat or Hill rate law (`2*MM kcat Km`, which BNG2.pl writes and #1230 will read), and the `Ele` keyword. None of 5,131 `.net` files on hand, from BNG2.pl and other writers, has such a line.
