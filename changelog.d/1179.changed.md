- **NFsim (`method='nf'` / `'nf_reject'`, `NfsimSession`) refuses a model
  whose rate laws read a linear table function indexed by time, naming the
  tables.** NFsim reads such a table only at its x values and after each
  reaction, and holds that value in between, so where little fires a ramping
  rate lags with no error: 100 `X(p~1)` leaving at a rate that ramps from 0 at
  t = 0 to 10 at t = 1 stayed at 100 through t = 1, where the exact mean is
  1.75. Simulate it with `method='rm'`, or with `method='ssa'` on the
  generated network, or make the table a step on a fine grid, which NFsim now
  reads exactly (#892). A table no rate law reads still runs. Lifted under
  #1179.
