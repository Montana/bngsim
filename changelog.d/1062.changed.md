- **NFsim (`method='nf'` / `'nf_reject'`, `NfsimSession`) refuses a function
  with an argument that reads a changing observable outside it, naming the
  function and the observable.** NFsim re-evaluated such a function only on the
  complexes a reaction produced, so in `fA(x) = k*(1+AC(x))*(1+0.01*MAB)`, or a
  rate law `fB(x)*g()` whose `g()` reads `MAB`, that term went stale on every
  other molecule and the bound count came out about 10 % low, without an error
  (RuleWorld/nfsim#104; lifted under #1062). Reads through the argument, reads
  of an observable no rule can change, and global rate laws still run. The
  network-free guide and `NfsimSession.set_param` also say that a write to a
  compartment-size parameter does not reach NFsim (#1061).
