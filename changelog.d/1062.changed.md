- **NFsim (`method='nf'` / `'nf_reject'`, `NfsimSession`) refuses a local
  function that reads a changing observable outside its argument, naming the
  function and the observable.** In `fA(x) = k*(1+AC(x))*(1+0.01*MAB)`, NFsim
  re-evaluated the `MAB` term only on the complexes a reaction produced, so it
  went stale on every other molecule and the bound count came out about 10 %
  low, without an error (RuleWorld/nfsim#104; lifted under #1062). A read of an
  observable that can never change, and every read through the argument, still
  run. The network-free guide and `NfsimSession.set_param` also say that a
  write to a compartment-size parameter does not reach NFsim (#1061).
