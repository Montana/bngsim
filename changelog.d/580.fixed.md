- **An SBML kinetic law's local parameter with no `value` is refused, like a global one** ([#580](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/580)). Level 2 loaded it as 0.0, so its reaction was silently switched off; Level 3 read it as NaN and failed in CVODE with a message about negative states. A local parameter its law reads now raises `UnderSpecifiedModelError` naming it and its reaction. `BNGSIM_ALLOW_UNSET_PARAMS=1` loads it at 0.0 with a warning, on both levels. One its law does not read loads as before.
