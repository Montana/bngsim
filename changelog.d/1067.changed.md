- **NFsim (`method='nf'` / `'nf_reject'`, `NfsimSession`) refuses a
  species-level compartment on a pattern, naming the observable or rule.**
  NFsim read a compartment only from seed species and molecules, so it ignored
  BioNetGen's `@EC:A()` prefix and the pattern matched every compartment,
  without an error (RuleWorld/nfsim#106; lifted under #1067): an observable
  counted every A (300 against 200), `@EC:A() -> 0` degraded the A in other
  compartments too, and `0 -> @EC:A2()` ran without its volume factor. The
  molecule-level form, `A()@EC`, still runs.
