- **A parameter named as one of a size jump's functions keeps its value.** The
  functions a size's jump read through a diamond of rules (`_szj_*`, #1145)
  took their names without looking at the model's: a parameter of the same
  name was bound to the function (#266), which wrote its own value over the
  parameter's at every step. They now take a name no parameter has, as the
  event resize's `_rsz_*` do. (#1124)
