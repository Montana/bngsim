- **A differenced steady-state sensitivity is checked entry by entry, and `Model.jacobian()` refuses what it warned about** ([#1002](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1002)). Where a difference quotient's columns are solved again at a species' own step or at half the first, an entry was confirmed if it moved by no more than 1e-4 of its column's largest entry, so a small entry could be far off and pass: with `jacobian="fd"`, `X + X -> X + X + G` at k = 1e-6 beside G at 1e8 returned dG\*/ds 6.6e-5 for 5.9e-5 (12% off), with no error. An entry is now held to 1e-4 of itself (1e-10 of its column's largest, at least). On the corpus with `jacobian="fd"`, 63 more of 621 steady states are refused: 5 with an entry more than 1e-3 off itself, 34 right ones. `Model.jacobian()` raises `SimulationError` by the same rule where it returned the matrix beside a warning.
