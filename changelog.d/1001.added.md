- **`steady_state(criterion=...)`** ([#1001](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1001)). `"scaled"` asks, besides BNG2.pl's `||f(y)||_2/n < tol`, that a run taken on from the state for `max_time` (at most 1e6) move no species by more than `rtol·|y_i| + atol_i` (the march's own tolerances, rtol at least 1e-10, atol_i at most rtol times the largest species), and that one from it kicked by a millionth of each species not let the kick grow tenfold. The Newton step from the state to the root, `δ = −J⁻¹f` on the reduced system, in the same tolerances, decides where the solve stops. `"residual"` is BNG2.pl's test alone. `steady_state_batch` takes the option too; results carry `criterion`, `newton_distance` and `continued_run_distance`.
