- **`steady_state(criterion="scaled")`: a convergence test in the model's own units** ([#1001](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1001)). BNG2.pl's test, `||f(y)||_2/n < tol`, is a rate in the model's units, so a model in molar units or a slow one passes it far from its steady state: `X' = -k·x·(x-0.3)·(x-1)` at a scale of 1e-9 came back `converged=True` at x = 0.894, 1.003 and 0.131 after one step, where every run ends at 1. Under `"scaled"` a state is accepted when the Newton step to the root, `δ = −J⁻¹f` on the reduced system, is within `rtol·|y_i| + atol_i` of every species (`atol="auto"`'s `atol_i`), and every start of that example ends at 1. A singular Jacobian is not certified unless `f = 0`, nor a differenced one across a rate law's jump; `mask=` says what is measured, not what moves; above 512 unknowns the solve stops by the residual test. `steady_state_batch` takes it too; the result adds `newton_distance` and `criterion`.
