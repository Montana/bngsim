- **`steady_state()` no longer steps over a rate-law window on the way to its steady state** ([#904](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/904), part 3). A rate law switched by a comparison over model state is constant on each side of the switch, so a step across a narrow window reads it as off at both ends. `run()` roots each such condition since #897, but the steady-state march built its own integrator and rooted nothing: with Y made at k while `0.5 < S < 0.505` and S decaying from 1, `steady_state()` returned Y = 0 with `converged=True`, where a run gives 0.199. The march, and each entry of `steady_state_batch()`, now roots the same conditions and restarts on each crossing. A state that slides along its threshold is rooted for 1000 crossings and then marched over the switch as before. New on the result: `n_state_switch_roots` and `state_switch_roots_dropped`.
